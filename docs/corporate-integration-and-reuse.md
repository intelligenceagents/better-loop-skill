# Corporate integration and reuse

Better Loop's public skill and local tooling remain under the [MIT license](https://github.com/intelligenceagents/better-loop-skill/blob/main/LICENSE). Keep the applicable copyright and permission notice in copies or substantial portions of the software. This policy does not add a condition to that license.

## Voluntary notice

We welcome personal, research and corporate reuse. If you integrate Better Loop into an internal system, distribute a modified version, or build another project with it, we invite you to tell us.

Use this repository's **Issues → New issue → Voluntary integration or reuse notice** form. If that form is unavailable, a general issue titled “Voluntary reuse notice” is sufficient. Notification is optional. You do not need permission, registration, a reply or an agreement with Better Loop to exercise the MIT permissions.

A useful notice contains the Better Loop version or commit, the broad kind of integration, an optional public project link, and any feedback you wish to share. You may omit your company name. The notice is public: do not include employee details, assessment results, private prompts, source documents, internal addresses, credentials or confidential business information. If you cannot describe the reuse publicly, omit the notice. Do not use an issue for a sensitive security report; follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/intelligenceagents/better-loop-skill/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

The skill sends no reuse notice automatically and adds no tracking or telemetry for this purpose. A notice does not grant Better Loop permission to publish a case study, use your logo, collect results or train on your work.

## Results sent to your own internal system

We call this an **internal integration**. Your chosen destination is operated under your own organization's authorization and data rules. The software license is separate from permission to process employee or company information.

The current Better Loop tooling does not supply a corporate uploader, hosted company workspace or employer dashboard. Its private reports remain on the originating device. An organization may build its own integration using the MIT software, with appropriate authorization and explicit control over the destination and fields it sends. Such an integration is not a Better Loop public contribution.

Publishing to Better Loop's public website remains a separate decision about an exact minimized candidate and its selected purposes. An internal integration or voluntary reuse notice does not provide that consent.
